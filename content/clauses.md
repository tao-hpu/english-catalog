## 一、什么是从句

**从句(Subordinate Clause)** = 一个有"主+谓"的句子,作为更大句子的一个成分。

### 基本概念

```
主句(Main Clause):能独立成句的部分
从句(Subordinate Clause):依附主句,做某个成分(主语/宾语/定语/状语)
```

**例:**
> **I know** [that you are tired].
>  主句          宾语从句

> **The book** [which I bought yesterday] **is interesting**.
>  主句         定语从句               主句续

### 三大从句类型

| 类型 | 作用 | 引导词举例 |
|------|------|----------|
| **名词性从句** | 当名词用(主/宾/表/同位语) | that, whether, if, what, who, when, why, how |
| **定语从句** | 修饰名词 | who, which, that, whose, when, where |
| **状语从句** | 修饰动词/句子(时间/地点/原因等) | when, because, although, if, so that... |

## 二、名词性从句

**名词性从句 = 当一个"大名词"用,放在句子里做主/宾/表/同位语。**

### 1️⃣ 主语从句

```
主语从句 + 谓语 + ...
或:It + 谓语 + ... + that 主语从句(更常用!)
```

> **What he said** is true.(他说的是真的)
> **That she will come** is certain.(她会来是确定的)
> **It is certain that** she will come.(更常用) ⭐

📌 **常用 it 形式主语句型:**
- It is + 形容词 + that ...(It is important that ...)
- It is + 名词 + that ...(It is a fact that ...)
- It is said / reported / believed that ...(据说/据报道/人们相信...)

### 2️⃣ 宾语从句 ⭐(最高频)

```
主语 + 谓语动词 + 宾语从句
```

> I think **(that) you are right**.
> She told me **that she was busy**.
> I don't know **whether / if he will come**.
> Tell me **what you want**.

⚠️ **关键规则:宾语从句要"主从时态一致"**

| 主句 | 从句通常 |
|------|---------|
| 现在时 | 任意时态 |
| 过去时 | **过去类**(过去 / 过去完成 / 过去将来) |

> She **says** she **is** busy.(主现 + 从现)
> She **said** she **was** busy.(主过 + 从过)
> She **said** she **had finished**.(主过 + 从过完)
> She **said** she **would come**.(主过 + 过将)

❗ **例外:从句是客观真理时,不变!**
> The teacher said the earth **is** round.(地球是圆的——客观真理)

### 3️⃣ 表语从句

```
主语 + be 动词 + 表语从句
```

> The problem **is that we have no money**.
> The reason **is that he was late**.
> This is **what I want**.

### 4️⃣ 同位语从句

**说明前面名词的具体内容**。常见名词:fact, idea, news, hope, belief, suggestion

> The fact **that he lied** shocked me.
> I have no idea **whether he will come**.
> The news **that we won** spread quickly.

⚠️ 同位语从句 vs 定语从句的区分:

| 类型 | 引导词 that 的作用 | 例 |
|------|------------------|-----|
| 同位语 | 仅起连接作用,不充当成分 | the fact **that he lied**(他撒谎=fact本身) |
| 定语从句 | 充当从句中的成分 | the fact **that he mentioned**(他提到的事实)|

### 5️⃣ that vs whether vs if

| 词 | 用法 |
|----|------|
| **that** | 引导陈述内容,没有疑问意味 |
| **whether** | 是否(陈述/疑问中性) |
| **if** | 只能在宾语从句中表"是否",其他位置必须用 whether |

> I know **that** he is tired.(我知道他累)
> I wonder **whether / if** he is tired.(我想知道他是否累)
> **Whether** he comes or not is unimportant.(主语从句必须用 whether)
> ❌ If he comes or not is unimportant.

### 6️⃣ 名词性从句的语序 ⭐ 最高频错误

**关键:名词性从句中用陈述语序,不用疑问语序!**

❌ I don't know **where is the station**.
✅ I don't know **where the station is**.

❌ Tell me **what does he want**.
✅ Tell me **what he wants**.

❌ I wonder **how old is she**.
✅ I wonder **how old she is**.

📌 **判断:** 这些"how old / where / what"已经是宾语从句的一部分,不是疑问句,所以**主谓不倒装**!

## 三、定语从句(关系从句)

**定语从句 = 修饰前面名词的句子。**

### 1️⃣ 基本结构

```
名词(先行词) + 关系词 + 句子
```

> the man **who lives next door**
>  先行词    定语从句

> the book **which I bought**
>  先行词    定语从句

### 2️⃣ 关系代词 / 关系副词

| 关系词 | 修饰 | 在从句中作 | 例 |
|-------|------|-----------|-----|
| **who** | 人 | 主语 | the man **who** called |
| **whom** | 人 | 宾语(正式) | the man **whom** I met |
| **whose** | 人/物 | 所有格 | the man **whose** car is red |
| **which** | 物 | 主语/宾语 | the book **which** I read |
| **that** | 人/物 | 主语/宾语 | the book **that** I read |
| **when** | 时间 | 状语 | the day **when** I met her |
| **where** | 地点 | 状语 | the place **where** I live |
| **why** | 原因 | 状语 | the reason **why** he left |

### 3️⃣ which / that / who 选择 ⭐

```
判断流程:

1. 先行词是什么?
   - 人 → who(主) / whom(宾) / that
   - 物 → which / that

2. 关系词在从句中作什么成分?
   - 主语 → who / which / that
   - 宾语 → whom / which / that(可省略)
   - 所有格 → whose
```

### 4️⃣ that 的特殊偏好场景

**只能用 that,不能用 which 的情况:**

| 情境 | 例 |
|------|-----|
| 先行词是 all / everything / nothing / something | All **that** glitters is not gold. |
| 先行词被序数词 / 最高级修饰 | the first thing **that** happened |
| 先行词被 the only / very / same 修饰 | the only person **that** I know |
| 先行词同时含人和物 | the people and things **that** I love |
| 疑问句以 who / which 开头 | Who **that** has heart could refuse? |

**只能用 which,不能用 that 的情况:**

| 情境 | 例 |
|------|-----|
| 非限制性定语从句(有逗号) | My father, **which**(❌)/ **who** is 50, ...(其实人用who)|
| 介词前置 | the book **in which** I found ... |

### 5️⃣ 限制性 vs 非限制性定语从句 ⭐

| 类型 | 标志 | 含义 |
|------|------|------|
| **限制性** | 无逗号 | 必不可少,删除则意思不全 |
| **非限制性** | 有逗号 | 补充说明,删除不影响主句意思 |

> The students **who study hard** will pass.(限制性——只有努力的会过)
> My brother, **who lives in Beijing**, is a doctor.(非限制性——补充说明,我只有一个哥哥)

⚠️ **非限制性定语从句:**
- 不能用 that
- 关系词不能省
- 用逗号隔开

### 6️⃣ 关系副词:when / where / why

**为什么用关系副词不用关系代词?**

> 看关系词在从句中是**作状语(when/where/why)**还是**作主/宾(which/that)**。

> the day **when** I met her = the day **on which** I met her
> the place **where** I work = the place **in which** I work
> the reason **why** he left = the reason **for which** he left

### 7️⃣ 关系代词省略

**可省略的情况:**
- 关系代词作宾语(限制性从句)
> the book (**that / which**) I read

**不可省的情况:**
- 关系代词作主语
> the man **who** called(不能省)
- 非限制性从句
> My father, **who** is 50, ...

## 四、状语从句

**状语从句 = 修饰动词或整个句子,表示时间/原因/条件等。**

### 1️⃣ 时间状语从句

| 引导词 | 含义 | 例 |
|-------|------|-----|
| **when** | 当...时(瞬间/同时) | I'll call you **when** I arrive. |
| **while** | 在...期间(持续动作) | He read **while** I cooked. |
| **as** | 当...时(同时) | **As** time passed, ... |
| **before / after** | 之前/之后 | Wash hands **before** eating. |
| **since** | 自从 | I've worked here **since** I graduated. |
| **until / till** | 直到 | Wait **until** I come back. |
| **as soon as** | 一...就 | I'll call **as soon as** I get home. |
| **the moment / the minute** | 一...就 | **The moment** I saw him, ... |
| **by the time** | 到...时为止 | **By the time** you arrive, I'll have left. |
| **whenever** | 每当 | **Whenever** I see you, ... |

⚠️ **重要规则:时间/条件状语从句中,用现在时表将来!**

❌ I will call you when **I will arrive**.
✅ I will call you when **I arrive**.

❌ If it **will rain**, I'll stay home.
✅ If it **rains**, I'll stay home.

### 2️⃣ 原因状语从句

| 引导词 | 含义 | 强弱 | 例 |
|-------|------|------|-----|
| **because** | 因为(回答 why) | 强 | I'm late **because** of traffic. |
| **since** | 既然(已知原因) | 弱 | **Since** you're here, let's start. |
| **as** | 由于 | 弱 | **As** it's late, we should leave. |
| **for** | 因为(连接前句) | 文 | He's tired, **for** he worked all night. |
| **now that** | 既然 | 弱 | **Now that** you've graduated, ... |

📌 **不能在同一句话中同时用 because 和 so:**
- ❌ Because it rained, so we stayed home.
- ✅ Because it rained, we stayed home.
- ✅ It rained, so we stayed home.

### 3️⃣ 条件状语从句

| 引导词 | 含义 | 例 |
|-------|------|-----|
| **if** | 如果 | **If** you study, you'll pass. |
| **unless** | 除非(=if not) | I'll go **unless** it rains. |
| **as long as** | 只要 | **As long as** you try, ... |
| **provided that / providing** | 假如 | **Provided that** all goes well, ... |
| **in case** | 万一 | Take an umbrella **in case** it rains. |
| **suppose / supposing** | 假设 | **Suppose** he doesn't come, ... |

### 4️⃣ 让步状语从句

| 引导词 | 含义 | 例 |
|-------|------|-----|
| **although / though** | 虽然 | **Although** he's young, he's wise. |
| **even though** | 即使 | **Even though** it rained, we went. |
| **even if** | 即使(假设) | **Even if** you ask, I won't tell. |
| **while** | 尽管(对比) | **While** I agree, I have concerns. |
| **whatever / whoever / however** | 无论...都 | **Whatever** happens, I'll support you. |
| **no matter what / how / when** | 无论 | **No matter how** hard it is, keep trying. |

⚠️ **although vs but 不能同时用:**
- ❌ **Although** he's poor, **but** he's happy.
- ✅ **Although** he's poor, he's happy.
- ✅ He's poor, **but** he's happy.

### 5️⃣ 目的状语从句

| 引导词 | 含义 | 例 |
|-------|------|-----|
| **so that** | 以便(常 + can/could/will/would) | I work hard **so that** I can pass. |
| **in order that** | 以便(正式) | She left early **in order that** she could catch the train. |
| **lest** | 以免 | He whispered **lest** he should wake the baby. |
| **in case** | 以防 | Take notes **in case** you forget. |

### 6️⃣ 结果状语从句

| 引导词 | 含义 | 例 |
|-------|------|-----|
| **so + 形容词 / 副词 + that** | 如此...以至于 | It was **so cold that** the lake froze. |
| **such + (a/an) + 形 + 名词 + that** | 如此...以至于 | It was **such a cold day that** ... |
| **so + 形容词 + a + 名 + that** | 同上(正式) | **so cold a day that** ... |

### 7️⃣ 比较状语从句

| 引导词 | 例 |
|-------|-----|
| **than** | She is taller **than** I (am). |
| **as ... as** | He is **as tall as** his father. |
| **the more ... the more** | **The more** you read, **the more** you learn. |

### 8️⃣ 方式状语从句

| 引导词 | 例 |
|-------|-----|
| **as** | Do **as** I say. |
| **as if / as though** | He talks **as if** he knew everything. |
| **the way** | Do it **the way** I showed you. |

## 五、that 何时绝对不能省略

> 📌 看曼尼台词时我们说过:`the class (that) I take` —— that 在定语从句里做**宾语**,可以省。但有几种情况 that **绝对不能省**,省了句子结构崩塌或产生严重歧义。这一节把"硬性规定"集中讲清。

### 🎯 一句话原则

> **that 能省的唯一安全场景:它在从句里做"宾语"(后面紧跟主语+动词)。**
> 其他情况——做主语、做骨架、防歧义——一律不能省。

### 1️⃣ 定语从句中:that 做"主语"时,不能省 ⭐

判断诀窍:**that 后面紧跟的是动词 → that 是主语 → 不能省。**

| ❌ 错误 | ✅ 正确 |
|--------|---------|
| The girl ~~spoke to you~~ is my sister. | The girl **that spoke** to you is my sister. |
| The book ~~is on the table~~ is mine. | The book **that is** on the table is mine. |

**对比记忆:**

```
the book (that) I read   → that 后是 "I"(主语)→ that 做宾语 → 可省 ✅
the book that is good     → that 后是 "is"(动词)→ that 做主语 → 不能省 ❌省
```

📌 省了主语,从句就"没骨架"了,会出现"两个谓语动词连着"的错乱(spoke / is)。

### 2️⃣ 名词性从句中:4 种情况必须保留 that

虽然宾语从句的 that 通常能省(`He said (that) he was tired`),但下面 4 种钉死不能省:

#### ① 主语从句位于句首

| ❌ 错误 | ✅ 正确 |
|--------|---------|
| He passed the exam surprised us. | **That** he passed the exam surprised us. |

句首需要 that "带路",否则读者会把 "He passed the exam" 误当成主句。

⚠️ 但用形式主语 it 把从句后置时,口语偶尔能省:
> It surprised us **(that)** he passed the exam.

#### ② 并列宾语从句:第一个能省,第二个起绝对不能省 ⭐(大坑!)

| 写法 | 问题 |
|------|------|
| ❌ He said (that) he was rich and he wanted a car. | "he wanted a car" 会被理解成与 "He said" 并列 → 变成"他说他有钱,而且(现在)他想买车" |
| ✅ He said (that) he was rich and **that** he wanted a car. | 第二个 that 像夹子,把第二件事也扣进"他说"的内容里 |

📌 这是中国学生写长句最容易出歧义的地方,务必记住:**并列从句,第二个 that 必留。**

#### ③ 同位语从句

解释抽象名词(news / fact / idea / rumor / belief)内容的 that,不能省——省了会和定语从句混淆。

> The news **that** our team won spread quickly.
> The fact **that** he lied shocked everyone.

#### ④ 主句与从句被状语/间接宾语"隔开"时

中间插了时间/地点状语或间接宾语,that 必须保留来提示"后面是新从句"。

> He told me yesterday **that** he would leave for Singapore.
> She promised, in front of everyone, **that** she would help.

### 3️⃣ 强调句型:It is / was ... that ... 中的 that 不能省

强调句固定公式:`It is / was + 被强调部分 + that + 其余部分`,that 是句型的半壁江山。

| ❌ 错误 | ✅ 正确 |
|--------|---------|
| It was in Beijing I met her. | It was in Beijing **that** I met her. |
| It is you should apologize. | It is you **that / who** should apologize. |

📌 强调人时可用 who 替代 that,强调物/时间/地点一般用 that。

### 💡 极简速记口诀

```
┌──────────────────────────────────────┐
│  定从做主不能省(后面动词离不开它)  │
│  句首主语不能省(句首需要它带路)    │
│  并列第二不能省(防止意思乱套)      │
│  强调、同位、被隔开,老实留着它!    │
│                                      │
│  唯一能省:定从/宾从里 that 做宾语   │
│  (that 后紧跟"主语+动词")          │
└──────────────────────────────────────┘
```

## 六、雅思高分从句应用

### 🎯 写作中三大从句的"组合拳"

**模板:复杂句 = 主句 + 多种从句**

> **Although** education is essential(让步状语), **what really matters**(主语从句) **is the way** [in which we apply knowledge](定语从句), **so that**(目的状语) we can contribute to society.

### 🎯 雅思口语中的"显高分从句"

**Part 2 描述类:**

> The reason **why I love this place** is **that it reminds me of my childhood**.
> What I find most fascinating is the way **(that) people interact there**.

**Part 3 议论类:**

> While some people believe **(that) technology improves our lives**, others argue **(that) it makes us lazier**.
> If we had invested more in education, we **would have** seen significant changes.

### 🎯 雅思写作 Task 2 模板

```
开头(总观点):
It is widely believed that [陈述论点 - 名词性从句].

让步:
Although [反方观点 - 让步状语], I am convinced that [自方观点].

支持论据(原因):
The main reason why [自方论点] is that [原因 - 名词性从句].

举例(定语从句):
Take students who study abroad as an example. They develop skills [that are essential in modern society].

结论:
In conclusion, [总结] - [让步从句 - 重申观点].
```

### 🎯 显示英语水平的"句法升级"

**简单句 → 复合句升级**

| 简单 | 升级(用从句) |
|------|------------|
| I'm tired. I'll sleep early. | **Because I'm tired**, I'll sleep early. |
| He didn't come. I was sad. | I was sad **that he didn't come**. |
| She is my friend. She lives next door. | She, **who lives next door**, is my friend. |
| The book is good. I'm reading it. | The book **(that) I'm reading** is good. |
| I'll call you. I'll arrive. | I'll call you **as soon as I arrive**. |
