## 一、核心概念

### 三大非谓语形式

| 形式 | 构成 | 主动/被动 | 时态意味 |
|------|------|----------|---------|
| **to do**(不定式) | to + 原形 | 主动 | 将来 / 抽象 |
| **doing**(动名词/现在分词) | -ing | 主动 | 正在进行 / 抽象 |
| **done**(过去分词) | -ed / 不规则 | 被动 | 已完成 |

### 一句话只能一个谓语

❌ I want **eat** dinner.(两个谓语)
✅ I want **to eat** dinner.

❌ He **finished** **wrote** the letter.
✅ He **finished writing** the letter.

❌ The boy **stand** there is my brother.(两个谓语)
✅ The boy **standing** there is my brother.

### 三种形式的"主动 vs 被动"

| 主动 | 被动 |
|------|------|
| to do | to be done |
| doing | being done |
| 已完成主动:having done | 已完成被动:having been done |

> A book **to read**(被读的书——这里不定式表被动?其实是主动逻辑:有书要读)
> A book **to be read**(将要被读的书——明确被动)
> The boy **breaking** the window(正在打破窗户的男孩——主动)
> The window **broken** by the boy(被男孩打破的窗——被动)

## 二、不定式 to do

### 1️⃣ 不定式作主语

> **To learn English** is important.(学英语很重要)
> 更常用形式主语 it:
> **It is important to learn English**. ⭐

### 2️⃣ 不定式作宾语

#### 后接 to do 的常见动词

```
want, hope, wish, plan, decide, agree, refuse, promise,
expect, offer, manage, fail, intend, learn, choose, pretend,
afford, attempt, dare, deserve, prepare, threaten, tend
```

> I **want to go**.
> She **decided to leave**.
> He **promised to help**.

#### 不定式作介词宾语?(几乎不行)

⚠️ 介词后不能用 to do!要用 doing!
- ❌ I'm interested **in to learn** English.
- ✅ I'm interested **in learning** English.

### 3️⃣ 不定式作宾语补足语 ⭐

```
S + V + 宾语 + to do
```

#### 后接"宾 + to do"的常见动词

```
ask, tell, want, expect, allow, advise, encourage, force,
persuade, invite, order, remind, warn, teach, help, get
```

> She **asked me to leave**.
> I **told him to wait**.
> They **expected us to win**.

⚠️ **特殊"使役 / 感官动词"后用原形,不用 to do!**
- make / let / have + 宾语 + **原形**
- see / hear / watch / feel + 宾语 + **原形**(完整)/ doing(进行)

> I **made him cry**.(不是 made him to cry)
> I **saw him leave**.(完整看到)
> I **saw him leaving**.(看到正在)

📌 **被动语态变回 to do!**
> They made him cry. → He **was made to cry**.
> I saw him leave. → He **was seen to leave**.

### 4️⃣ 不定式作目的状语 ⭐

> I came here **to see you**.(为了见你而来)
> He works hard **to support his family**.

📌 **强调目的:**
- in order to do = so as to do = to do(以便)
- 否定:in order **not** to / so as **not** to / so that ... not

### 5️⃣ 不定式作定语

```
名词 + to do(将要做的)
```

> I have a lot of work **to do**.
> Give me something **to drink**.
> The first person **to arrive** was John.

### 6️⃣ 不定式的固定句型

| 句型 | 例 |
|------|-----|
| **It is + 形 + (for sb) + to do** | **It is hard for me to wake up early**. |
| **It is + 形 + of sb + to do**(品质形容词) | **It is kind of you to help**. |
| **too + 形 + to do**(太...而不能) | He is **too young to drive**. |
| **形 + enough + to do**(足够...能...) | She is **old enough to drive**. |
| **so + 形 + as to do** | He was **so kind as to help**. |
| **疑问词 + to do** | I don't know **what to do / how to do it**. |
| **be + to do** | We **are to leave** tomorrow.(预定/义务) |

⚠️ **for vs of 区别:**
- It's **kind** of you = 形容人的品质 → of
- It's **hard / easy** for me = 客观难易 → for

## 三、动名词 doing

**动名词 = 动词当名词用,加 -ing。**

### 1️⃣ 动名词作主语

> **Smoking** is bad for health.
> **Learning English** takes time.

### 2️⃣ 动名词作宾语

#### 只能后接 doing 的动词 ⭐(必背!)

```
finish, enjoy, mind, suggest, avoid, consider, miss, deny,
imagine, admit, practice, postpone, delay, escape, give up,
keep, can't help, can't stand, look forward to, be used to,
be worth, feel like
```

记忆口诀:
> **enjoy / finish / mind / suggest / avoid / consider** — 这六个最高频!

> I **enjoy reading**.(❌ enjoy to read)
> She **finished writing**.(❌ finished to write)
> He **avoided meeting** me.(❌ avoided to meet)
> Would you **mind opening** the window?

#### 介词后必接 doing(超高频陷阱!)

> I'm interested **in learning**.
> Thanks **for helping**.
> She insisted **on paying**.
> Look forward **to seeing** you.(to 是介词,不是不定式!)
> Be used **to doing** sth(习惯做)
> Be addicted **to doing** sth(沉迷做)

⚠️ **关键混淆:to 是介词还是不定式标志?**

| to + doing(to 是介词) | to + do(to 是不定式) |
|---------------------|--------------------|
| look forward to doing | want to do |
| be used to doing(习惯) | used to do(过去常常) |
| be accustomed to doing | hope to do |
| object to doing | plan to do |
| confess to doing | decide to do |
| pay attention to doing | learn to do |
| devote oneself to doing | manage to do |

> ✅ I look forward to **seeing** you.
> ✅ I look forward to **meeting** you.
> ❌ I look forward to **see** you.

### 3️⃣ 动名词作表语

> My hobby is **reading**.
> Her job is **teaching English**.

⚠️ **区分:动名词作表语 vs 现在进行时**
- My hobby **is reading**.(reading 是表语,描述爱好)
- She **is reading** now.(reading 是进行时的一部分)

### 4️⃣ 动名词作定语(表用途)

> a **swimming** pool(游泳池——用来游泳的)
> a **sleeping** bag(睡袋——用来睡觉的)
> a **waiting** room(等候室)

⚠️ **区分动名词定语 vs 现在分词定语**
- a **sleeping** bag = a bag for sleeping(用途——动名词)
- a **sleeping** baby = a baby who is sleeping(主动正在做——现在分词)

### 5️⃣ 动名词作介词宾语

(已在上面说过,这里重申:介词后必须用 doing)

> She is good **at swimming**.
> Thank you **for coming**.
> Instead **of complaining**, do something.

## 四、现在分词 doing(作定语/状语)

**现在分词 doing**:表"主动 / 正在进行"

### 1️⃣ 现在分词作定语

#### 单个分词放名词前

> a **running** dog(正在跑的狗)
> a **crying** baby(哭着的婴儿)
> the **rising** sun(升起的太阳)

#### 分词短语放名词后(后置定语)⭐

```
名词 + doing + 其他成分
```

> The boy **standing at the door** is my brother.
> = The boy **who is standing at the door** is my brother.

> People **living in cities** often feel stressed.
> = People **who live in cities** ...

📌 **简化定语从句:** 主动正在进行 → doing

### 2️⃣ 现在分词作状语 ⭐(雅思高分必备!)

```
Doing ..., 主句

或 主句, doing ...
```

> **Walking down the street**, I saw an old friend.
> (= While I was walking down the street, ...)

> She sat there, **reading a book**.
> (= She sat there and was reading a book.)

#### 分词作状语的逻辑主语 = 主句主语!

⚠️ **悬垂分词错误**(逻辑主语不一致):

❌ **Walking down the street**, a dog barked at me.
(走在街上的不是狗!)

✅ **Walking down the street**, I heard a dog bark at me.

✅ **As I was walking down the street**, a dog barked at me.

### 3️⃣ 现在分词的功能

| 功能 | 例 |
|------|-----|
| 时间状语 | **Hearing the news**, she cried. |
| 原因状语 | **Being tired**, I went to bed. |
| 伴随状语 | He left, **slamming the door**. |
| 结果状语 | The rain stopped, **leaving the streets wet**. |
| 条件状语 | **Working hard**, you'll succeed. |

### 4️⃣ 完成式分词 having done

**强调动作发生在主句之前**

> **Having finished my homework**, I went out.
> (做完作业才出去)

> **Having lived here for years**, I know this place well.

### 5️⃣ 被动分词 being done / having been done

> **Being asked**, he didn't answer.(被问的时候)
> **Having been warned**, he was careful.(被警告过后)

## 五、过去分词 done

**过去分词 done**:表"被动 / 已完成"

### 1️⃣ 过去分词作定语

#### 单个分词放名词前

> a **broken** window(被打破的窗户)
> a **stolen** car(被偷的车)
> a **retired** teacher(已退休的老师)
> **fallen** leaves(落叶——已经落下)

#### 分词短语放名词后

> The book **written by him** is famous.
> = The book **which was written by him** is famous.

> Students **selected for the program** will receive funding.

### 2️⃣ 过去分词作状语

```
Done ..., 主句
```

> **Asked about her plan**, she smiled.(被问到——被动)
> **Built in 1900**, the building is still standing.

⚠️ 过去分词作状语 = 被动语态省略 + 主语一致

### 3️⃣ 现在分词 vs 过去分词作定语

| -ing(主动 / 进行) | -ed(被动 / 已完成) |
|------------------|------------------|
| a **boring** movie(电影让人无聊) | a **bored** student(学生感到无聊) |
| **falling** leaves(正在飘落) | **fallen** leaves(已落下) |
| a **smiling** boy(正在笑) | a **smiling**(无对应,人不能"被微笑") |
| a **developing** country(发展中) | a **developed** country(已发展的) |

### 4️⃣ 过去分词作宾语补足语

```
S + V + O + done
```

> I had my hair **cut**.(让...被剪)
> She got her car **repaired**.
> I want this done by tomorrow.

📌 **使役结构:**
- have / get + 宾 + done = 让...被做
- I had my photo **taken**.(我拍了照——别人给我拍)

## 六、独立主格结构

**独立主格 = 分词逻辑主语与主句主语不同时,显式带主语。**

### 结构

```
名词/代词 + 分词(doing / done) + ...,主句
```

### 例

> **The weather being fine**, we went hiking.
> (天气好,我们去远足——独立主语 the weather)

> **All the work done**, we left the office.
> (工作都做完,我们下班)

> **There being no bus**, we walked home.
> (没公交,我们走路回家)

> **He listening attentively**, I continued the story.

### 雅思高分用法

> **Time permitting**, I'll visit you.(如果时间允许)
> **Weather permitting**, the picnic will go ahead.
> **All things considered**, the plan is feasible.

## 七、雅思高分用法

### 🎯 高分句型 1:分词作状语开头

❌ **简单**:Because I was tired, I slept early.
✅ **升级**:**Tired**, I slept early. / **Being tired**, I slept early.

❌ **简单**:When I saw him, I waved.
✅ **升级**:**Seeing him**, I waved.

### 🎯 高分句型 2:简化定语从句

❌ **冗长**:The students who are studying in the library are quiet.
✅ **简洁**:The students **studying** in the library are quiet.

❌ **冗长**:The book that was written by him is famous.
✅ **简洁**:The book **written** by him is famous.

### 🎯 高分句型 3:不定式作目的(不要总用 because)

> She studies hard **to achieve her dreams**.(更高级)
> 比 because she wants to achieve her dreams 更简洁

### 🎯 高分句型 4:动名词作主语

> **Learning a foreign language** opens up a new world.
> **Being honest** is the best policy.

### 🎯 高分句型 5:独立主格

> **All things considered**, the proposal is reasonable.
> **Other factors being equal**, more education leads to higher income.

### 🎯 雅思 Task 2 万能模板(用非谓语)

```
开头:Studying abroad, many believe, broadens one's horizons.
   (动名词主语)

中间:Faced with growing competition, students must develop new skills
     to thrive in the global market.
     (过去分词状语 + 不定式目的)

结尾:Taking everything into account, education remains the key
     to social progress.
     (现在分词作状语)
```

## 🌟 一页速记总结

```
┌────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  非谓语三兄弟:                             │
│  to do  → 将来 / 主动 / 抽象               │
│  doing  → 正在 / 主动 / 习惯               │
│  done   → 被动 / 已完成                    │
│                                            │
│  关键规则:                                 │
│  1. 介词后必接 doing(不接 to do)         │
│  2. enjoy/finish/mind/avoid 后接 doing     │
│  3. want/hope/decide/promise 后接 to do    │
│  4. make/let/have + 宾 + 原形(无 to)     │
│  5. 分词作状语,逻辑主语必须 = 主句主语    │
│                                            │
│  关键陷阱:                                 │
│  - look forward to + doing(to 是介词!)   │
│  - be used to + doing(习惯)               │
│  - used to + do(过去常常)                 │
│                                            │
│  雅思高分:                                 │
│  - 用 doing/done 替代定语从句              │
│  - 用 doing 作状语开头(Walking..., I...) │
│  - 独立主格(All things considered, ...)   │
└────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```
