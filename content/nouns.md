## 一、名词:可数 vs 不可数

### 1️⃣ 核心区分

| 类型 | 特征 | 例子 |
|------|------|------|
| **可数名词(Countable)** | 能数 1、2、3 | book, apple, idea, student |
| **不可数名词(Uncountable)** | 不能直接数,看作整体 | water, advice, information, money |

### 2️⃣ 中国学生最容易错的"不可数名词"⭐

这些词在中文里能数,但英文里**不能数**:

| 不可数 | ❌ 错误 | ✅ 正确 |
|--------|--------|---------|
| **advice**(建议) | an advice / advices | a piece of advice / some advice |
| **information**(信息) | an information | a piece of information |
| **news**(消息) | a news / many news | a piece of news / much news |
| **furniture**(家具) | a furniture / furnitures | a piece of furniture |
| **luggage**(行李) | a luggage / luggages | a piece of luggage |
| **equipment**(设备) | an equipment | a piece of equipment |
| **homework**(作业) | a homework / homeworks | some homework |
| **research**(研究) | a research / researches | a piece of research |
| **knowledge**(知识) | a knowledge | knowledge / some knowledge |
| **progress**(进步) | a progress | progress / some progress |
| **traffic**(交通) | a traffic / traffics | heavy traffic |
| **bread**(面包) | a bread / breads | a slice of bread |
| **money**(钱) | a money / moneys | some money |

📌 **记忆口诀:** 抽象概念 + 集合名词 + 液体/粉末状物质 → 通常不可数

### 3️⃣ 既可数又不可数的"双面词"

| 词 | 不可数(整体/物质) | 可数(种类/个体) |
|----|-------------------|----------------|
| **coffee** | I drink coffee.(咖啡) | Two coffees, please.(两杯咖啡) |
| **time** | I have no time.(时间) | I've been there three times.(次数) |
| **chicken** | I eat chicken.(鸡肉) | A chicken is in the yard.(一只鸡) |
| **paper** | I need paper.(纸) | Read the paper.(报纸/论文) |
| **experience** | I have experience.(经验) | I had a strange experience.(一次经历) |

### 4️⃣ 单复数特殊变化

| 类型 | 规则 | 例子 |
|------|------|------|
| 单复同形 | 不变 | sheep, fish, deer, species, means |
| 只用复数 | 永远复数 | scissors, glasses, pants, jeans, clothes |
| 复数变化特殊 | 不规则 | child→children, foot→feet, mouse→mice, person→people |
| -s 结尾但单数 | 看似复数 | news, mathematics, physics, economics |

⚠️ **The news is good.**(news 是单数)

## 二、冠词:a / an / the / 零冠词

### 1️⃣ 决策树

```
要表达的名词 →

1. 是不是"特指"那个东西?(双方都知道指哪个)
   → YES: 用 the
   → NO: 进入第 2 步

2. 是单数可数名词吗?
   → YES: 用 a / an(泛指,任意一个)
   → NO(复数 / 不可数): 用零冠词(不加)
```

### 2️⃣ a vs an

**关键:** 看**发音**,不是看字母!

| 用 a | 用 an |
|------|-------|
| a book | an apple |
| a university(读 /juː/) | an hour(h 不发音) |
| a European | an honest man |
| a one-way ticket(读 /wʌn/) | an MBA(读 /em/) |

### 3️⃣ the 的核心用法

| 情境 | 例子 |
|------|------|
| 上文提到过 | I saw a cat. **The** cat was black. |
| 双方都知道 | Close **the** door.(此刻这扇门) |
| 独一无二 | **the** sun, **the** moon, **the** Earth |
| 形容词最高级 | **the** best, **the** tallest |
| 序数词 | **the** first, **the** second |
| 江河海洋山脉群岛 | **the** Yangtze, **the** Pacific, **the** Alps |
| 国家(复数/有联邦) | **the** US, **the** UK, **the** Netherlands |
| 乐器 | play **the** piano |
| 形容词指一类人 | **the** rich, **the** poor, **the** elderly |

### 4️⃣ 零冠词(不加)

| 情境 | 例子 |
|------|------|
| 复数泛指 | I love **dogs**.(泛指狗) |
| 不可数泛指 | **Water** is essential. |
| 国家、城市、人名 | China, Beijing, Tony |
| 三餐 | have **breakfast**, after **lunch** |
| 学科 | study **English**, learn **math** |
| 交通方式(by) | by **bus**, by **car**, by **plane** |
| 球类运动 | play **football**, play **basketball** |
| 表"功能"的地点 | go to **school**(去上学), go to **bed**(去睡觉) |

⚠️ **对比:**
- go to **school**(上学,目的) vs go to **the school**(去那栋学校建筑)
- go to **bed**(睡觉) vs sit on **the bed**(坐在床上)

## 三、代词系统

### 1️⃣ 人称代词 / 物主代词 / 反身代词总表

| 主格 | 宾格 | 形容词性物主 | 名词性物主 | 反身代词 |
|------|------|------------|----------|---------|
| I | me | my | mine | myself |
| you | you | your | yours | yourself / yourselves |
| he | him | his | his | himself |
| she | her | her | hers | herself |
| it | it | its | its | itself |
| we | us | our | ours | ourselves |
| they | them | their | theirs | themselves |

### 2️⃣ 形容词性 vs 名词性物主代词

| 形式 | 后接 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| **形容词性**(my, your...) | + 名词 | This is **my** book. |
| **名词性**(mine, yours...) | 单独使用 | This book is **mine**. |

❌ This is **mine** book.
✅ This is **my** book. / This book is **mine**.

### 3️⃣ 反身代词三大用法

**1. 动作返回主语**
> I hurt **myself**.(我伤了自己)
> She taught **herself** English.(她自学英语)

**2. 强调"亲自"**
> The president **himself** attended.(总统本人出席)
> I'll do it **myself**.

**3. 固定搭配**
- by oneself = 独自 / 单独
- enjoy oneself = 玩得开心
- help oneself to = 自取(食物)
- teach oneself = 自学

### 4️⃣ 指示代词:this / that / these / those

| 词 | 用于 | 距离 | 例 |
|----|------|------|-----|
| **this** | 单数 | 近 | This is my friend. |
| **that** | 单数 | 远 | That is the building. |
| **these** | 复数 | 近 | These books are mine. |
| **those** | 复数 | 远 | Those people are tourists. |

📌 **替代用法**(避免重复):
> The weather in Beijing is colder than **that** in Shanghai.
> (that = the weather)
> The buildings in this city are taller than **those** in my hometown.

### 5️⃣ 不定代词 ⭐ 雅思高频

#### some / any 的区别

| 词 | 用于 | 例 |
|----|------|-----|
| **some** | 肯定句 / 期望肯定回答的疑问句 | I have **some** money. / Would you like **some** tea? |
| **any** | 否定句 / 一般疑问 | I don't have **any** money. / Do you have **any** ideas? |

#### -body / -one / -thing 系列

| 系列 | 人 | 物 | 地点 |
|------|-----|----|----|
| every- | everybody / everyone | everything | everywhere |
| some- | somebody / someone | something | somewhere |
| any- | anybody / anyone | anything | anywhere |
| no- | nobody / no one | nothing | nowhere |

⚠️ **重点:**
- 这些词都是**单数**!→ Everybody **is** here.(不是 are)
- 形容词放**后面** → something **interesting**(不是 interesting something)

#### few / a few / little / a little ⭐

| 词 | 修饰 | 含义 | 情感色彩 |
|----|------|------|---------|
| **few** | 可数 | 几乎没有 | 否定 |
| **a few** | 可数 | 有几个 | 肯定 |
| **little** | 不可数 | 几乎没有 | 否定 |
| **a little** | 不可数 | 有一点 | 肯定 |

> I have **few** friends here.(几乎没朋友——孤独)
> I have **a few** friends here.(有几个朋友——还行)
> There's **little** hope.(几乎没希望)
> There's **a little** hope.(还有点希望)

#### each vs every

| 词 | 强调 | 后接 |
|----|------|------|
| **each** | 强调个体(两个或更多) | + 单数名词 |
| **every** | 强调整体(三个或更多) | + 单数名词 |

> **Each** student has a book.(每个学生各自)
> **Every** student loves the teacher.(所有学生都)

## 四、常见混淆点

### ❓ 混淆点 1:its vs it's

| 词 | 含义 | 例 |
|----|------|-----|
| **its** | 它的(物主) | The dog wagged **its** tail. |
| **it's** | it is / it has 缩写 | **It's** raining. / **It's** been a long day. |

### ❓ 混淆点 2:their / there / they're

| 词 | 含义 | 例 |
|----|------|-----|
| **their** | 他们的 | **Their** house is big. |
| **there** | 那里 / there is | **There** is a book. / Look over **there**. |
| **they're** | they are 缩写 | **They're** my friends. |

### ❓ 混淆点 3:much vs many

| 词 | 修饰 | 例 |
|----|------|-----|
| **many** | 可数复数 | many books |
| **much** | 不可数 | much water |

📌 **a lot of / lots of** 两者都能修饰,日常更常用。

### ❓ 混淆点 4:other / another / others / the other

| 词 | 用法 | 例 |
|----|------|-----|
| **another** | 再一个(三个以上中再一个) | I want **another** cup. |
| **the other** | 两个中的另一个 | One is red, **the other** is blue. |
| **other + 复数** | 别的(还有更多) | **other** people |
| **others** | 别的人/物(代词) | Some agree, **others** disagree. |
| **the others** | 剩下所有的 | Two left, **the others** stayed. |

## 五、雅思高频陷阱

### ⚠️ 陷阱 1:漏冠词

写作中最常扣分:

❌ I went to library yesterday.
✅ I went to **the** library yesterday.

❌ She is teacher.
✅ She is **a** teacher.

### ⚠️ 陷阱 2:复数 -s 漏掉

❌ Many student like English.
✅ Many student**s** like English.

❌ There are five book on the table.
✅ There are five book**s** on the table.

### ⚠️ 陷阱 3:不可数当可数用

❌ I have many homeworks to do.
✅ I have **a lot of homework** to do.

❌ He gave me an advice.
✅ He gave me **some advice / a piece of advice**.

### ⚠️ 陷阱 4:主谓一致

| 主语 | 谓语 |
|------|------|
| Everyone / Everybody | **is**(单数!) |
| The number of students | **is**(强调数量) |
| A number of students | **are**(强调学生) |
| News / Mathematics | **is**(虽-s,但单数) |
| The police | **are**(集合复数) |

### ⚠️ 陷阱 5:代词指代不清

❌ Tom told Jack that he was wrong.(he 是谁?)
✅ Tom told Jack, "**You** are wrong." / Tom admitted to Jack that **he himself** was wrong.
