## 一、时间介词:in / on / at

### 决策树

```
时间长度 → 介词

长(月/年/季/世纪)  → in
中(具体某天/日期)  → on
短(具体时刻/时点)  → at
```

### 详细对照表

| 介词 | 用于 | 例 |
|------|------|-----|
| **in** | 月、年、季节、世纪、世纪、上午/下午/晚上 | in May, in 2026, in summer, in the 21st century, in the morning |
| **on** | 具体日期、星期、节日 | on Monday, on May 10, on Christmas Day, on my birthday |
| **at** | 具体时刻、夜间、节日(用餐/活动) | at 3 o'clock, at noon, at midnight, at night, at Christmas |

### ⚠️ 容易错的对比

| ✅ 正确 | ❌ 常见错误 |
|--------|-----------|
| **in** the morning | on the morning |
| **at** night | in the night |
| **on** Monday morning(具体日期+morning) | in Monday morning |
| **on** my birthday | at my birthday |
| **at** Christmas(节日活动期间) | in Christmas |
| **on** Christmas Day(具体那天) | in Christmas Day |
| **in** 2026(年) | on 2026 |
| **in** the past(过去) | at the past |

### 时间介词其他用法

| 介词 | 含义 | 例 |
|------|------|-----|
| **for** | 持续多久(+ 时间段) | I've lived here **for** 5 years. |
| **since** | 从...以来(+ 时间点) | I've lived here **since** 2020. |
| **during** | 在...期间 | I slept **during** the meeting. |
| **by** | 截止到(不晚于) | Finish it **by** Friday. |
| **until / till** | 直到 | I'll wait **until** 6 pm. |
| **from...to** | 从...到 | from 9 to 5 |
| **before / after** | 之前 / 之后 | before lunch, after work |
| **within** | 在...之内 | within 24 hours |

⚠️ **by vs until:**
- **by** = 不晚于(动作在那时间点前完成即可)
> Finish it **by** Friday.(周五前完成都行)
- **until** = 直到(动作持续到那一刻)
> I worked **until** midnight.(一直工作到半夜)

## 二、地点介词:in / on / at

### 决策树

```
范围大小 → 介词

大(国家/城市/区域)  → in
中(街道/海岸线/平面)→ on
小(具体地点/某点)   → at
```

### 详细对照表

| 介词 | 用于 | 例 |
|------|------|-----|
| **in** | 国家、城市、房间、容器内 | in China, in Beijing, in the kitchen, in the box |
| **on** | 街道、海岸、平面、楼层、交通工具(public) | on Wall Street, on the coast, on the table, on the 3rd floor, on the bus |
| **at** | 具体地点、地址、活动 | at the airport, at home, at 123 Main St., at the meeting |

### ⚠️ 关键区分

#### in vs on:容器 vs 表面
- **in** the box(在盒子里)
- **on** the box(在盒子上)
- **in** the wall(嵌在墙里)
- **on** the wall(挂在墙上)

#### at vs in:点 vs 内部
- **at** the school(在学校这个点/地址)
- **in** the school(在学校建筑里面)
- **at** home(在家——把家看作一个地点)
- **in** the house(在房子里——空间)

#### 交通工具的介词

| 交通工具 | 介词 |
|---------|------|
| in a car / taxi(私家小车) | **in** |
| on a bus / train / plane / ship / bike(公共/大型) | **on** |
| by car / bus / train / plane(强调方式) | **by** + 零冠词 |

> I came **by** car.(乘车来——方式)
> I sat **in** the car.(在车里——位置)

#### 地址介词

> I live **on** Park Street.(住在 Park 街上)
> I live **at** 123 Park Street.(住在具体门牌)
> I live **in** New York.(住在城市)

## 三、方位介词

### 视觉化对比

| 介词 | 含义 | 例 |
|------|------|-----|
| **in** | 在...里面 | The cat is in the box. |
| **on** | 在...上面(接触) | The book is on the table. |
| **above** | 在...上方(不接触) | The lamp is above the table. |
| **over** | 在...正上方 / 越过 | The bird flew over the house. |
| **under** | 在...下方 | The ball is under the chair. |
| **below** | 在...下面(不一定正下方) | His name is below mine on the list. |
| **between** | 在(两者)之间 | between A and B |
| **among** | 在(三者以上)之中 | among friends |
| **next to / beside** | 紧挨着 | She sat next to me. |
| **near** | 在...附近 | I live near the station. |
| **in front of** | 在...前面 | A car is in front of the house. |
| **behind** | 在...后面 | A garden is behind the house. |
| **opposite** | 在...对面 | The bank is opposite the post office. |
| **across** | 横穿 / 在对面 | walk across the street |
| **through** | 穿过(内部) | walk through the forest |
| **along** | 沿着 | walk along the river |
| **around** | 围绕 / 大约 | sit around the table |
| **into** | 进入 | walk into the room |
| **out of** | 走出 | get out of the car |
| **toward(s)** | 朝向 | walk toward the door |
| **from / to** | 从...到... | from A to B |

### ⚠️ 常混淆

#### between vs among
- **between**:两者之间(也可用于多者间互相)
- **among**:三者以上的群体中

> Choose **between** A and B.(两个之间选)
> She is popular **among** her classmates.(在群体中)

#### across vs through
- **across**:横穿表面(街道、河面)
- **through**:穿过内部(森林、隧道、人群)

> walk **across** the street(横穿马路)
> walk **through** the tunnel(穿过隧道)

## 四、其他高频介词

### with / without

| 用法 | 例 |
|------|-----|
| 伴随、用具 | I cut it **with** a knife. |
| 拥有特征 | a girl **with** blonde hair |
| 没有 | I can't live **without** you. |

### by

| 用法 | 例 |
|------|-----|
| 被动语态(执行者) | written **by** Shakespeare |
| 方式 | **by** train, **by** email |
| 旁边 | sit **by** the window |
| 截止时间 | finish **by** Friday |

### for

| 用法 | 例 |
|------|-----|
| 为了 | This is **for** you. |
| 持续时间 | **for** two hours |
| 原因 | famous **for** its beauty |
| 交换 | I paid $10 **for** the book. |

### of

| 用法 | 例 |
|------|-----|
| 所属(无生命物) | the leg **of** the table |
| 关于 | think **of** you |
| 由...构成 | a glass **of** water |
| 出自 | one **of** my friends |

### about / on(关于)

| 用法 | 例 |
|------|-----|
| **about**(一般"关于") | a book **about** dogs |
| **on**(更专业、正式) | a lecture **on** physics |

### despite / in spite of(尽管)

> **Despite** the rain, we went out.
> **In spite of** his age, he runs fast.

⚠️ **despite** / **in spite of** 后接**名词或动名词**,不能接句子。
- ❌ Despite it rained, ...
- ✅ **Although** it rained, ...
- ✅ **Despite** the rain, ...

### due to / because of(因为)

> The match was canceled **due to / because of** the rain.

⚠️ 后接名词,不接句子。表"原因"的句子用 **because**。

## 六、雅思高频短语动词

短语动词 = 动词 + 副词 / 介词,意思常常**不能字面理解**。

### 1️⃣ 高频生活类

| 短语动词 | 含义 | 例 |
|---------|------|-----|
| **get up** | 起床 | I get up at 7. |
| **wake up** | 醒来 | I woke up at 6. |
| **stay up** | 熬夜 | I stayed up late. |
| **put on** | 穿上 / 戴上 | put on a coat |
| **take off** | 脱下 / 飞机起飞 | take off your shoes |
| **turn on / off** | 开 / 关(电器) | turn on the TV |
| **turn up / down** | 调高 / 调低(音量) | turn up the volume |
| **look up** | 查阅 | look up a word |
| **find out** | 查出 / 弄清 | find out the truth |
| **hand in** | 上交 | hand in homework |

### 2️⃣ 高频学习/工作类

| 短语动词 | 含义 | 例 |
|---------|------|-----|
| **carry out** | 实施 / 执行 | carry out a plan |
| **point out** | 指出 | point out mistakes |
| **figure out** | 想出 / 弄明白 | figure out a solution |
| **work out** | 解决 / 算出 | work out the answer |
| **come up with** | 想出 / 提出 | come up with an idea |
| **bring up** | 提出 / 抚养 | bring up a question |
| **set up** | 建立 | set up a company |
| **take up** | 开始(爱好/职业) | take up painting |
| **give up** | 放弃 | give up smoking |
| **keep up with** | 跟上 | keep up with the class |
| **catch up with** | 赶上 | catch up with friends |
| **deal with** | 处理 | deal with problems |
| **focus on** | 专注于 | focus on studies |

### 3️⃣ 高频社交类

| 短语动词 | 含义 | 例 |
|---------|------|-----|
| **get along with** | 与...相处 | get along with classmates |
| **break up** | 分手 | They broke up. |
| **make up** | 和好 / 编造 | make up after a fight |

| 短语动词 | 含义 | 用在 |
|---------|------|-----|

| **carry out** | 进行(研究/调查) | carry out research |
| **come about** | 发生 | how it came about |

| **result in** | 导致 | result in failure |
| **stem from** | 源于 | problems stem from poverty |
| **account for** | 占 / 解释 | accounts for 30% |
| **rely on / depend on** | 依靠 | rely on technology |
| **contribute to** | 促成 / 造成 | contribute to society |
| **call for** | 需要 / 呼吁 | call for action |
| **cope with** | 应对 | cope with stress |
