## 一、被动语态:概念与构造

### 1️⃣ 主动 vs 被动

```
主动:主语 + 动词 + 宾语
       (主语做动作)

被动:主语 + be + 过去分词 + (by 动作发出者)
       (主语承受动作)
```

| 主动 | 被动 |
|------|------|
| Tom **wrote** the book. | The book **was written** (by Tom). |
| She **cleans** the room. | The room **is cleaned** (by her). |
| They **will build** a school. | A school **will be built**. |

### 2️⃣ 何时用被动语态?

**1. 不知道动作发出者**
> My phone **was stolen**.(谁偷的不知道)

**2. 动作发出者不重要**
> English **is spoken** here.(谁说不重要)

**3. 强调动作的承受者**
> The **Mona Lisa** was painted by da Vinci.(强调画)

**4. 客观、正式的写作风格**(雅思写作高频)
> It **is widely believed that** ...
> The experiment **was conducted** in 2020.

**5. 避免点名**(委婉)
> Mistakes **were made**.(避免说"我们犯了错")

### 3️⃣ 被动语态的核心公式

```
       be(根据时态变化) + 过去分词

主动 → 被动 三步走:
1. 主动宾语 → 被动主语
2. 动词改成 "be + 过去分词"(be 与原时态匹配)
3. 主动主语 → "by + 主动主语"(可省略)
```

**例:**
> Tom (主语) wrote (谓语) the book (宾语). [一般过去]
> ↓
> The book (主语) was written (be过去 + 过分) by Tom.

## 二、被动语态:八大时态变化

以 **write a book** 为例:

| 时态 | 主动 | 被动 |
|------|------|------|
| 一般现在 | writes a book | **is written** |
| 一般过去 | wrote a book | **was written** |
| 一般将来 | will write a book | **will be written** |
| 现在进行 | is writing a book | **is being written** |
| 过去进行 | was writing a book | **was being written** |
| 现在完成 | has written a book | **has been written** |
| 过去完成 | had written a book | **had been written** |
| 情态动词 | should write a book | **should be written** |

### 万能公式

```
任何时态被动 = 该时态的 be + 过去分词

                              be 形式
一般现在     is/am/are
一般过去     was/were
一般将来     will be
现在进行     is/am/are being
过去进行     was/were being
现在完成     has/have been
过去完成     had been
情态被动     should/can/must be
不定式被动   to be done
动名词被动   being done
```

### ⚠️ 容易出错的被动

#### ❌ 错误 1:漏 be

❌ The book **wrote** by him.
✅ The book **was written** by him.

#### ❌ 错误 2:用了原形而非过去分词

❌ The room is **clean** every day.(clean 是原形)
✅ The room is **cleaned** every day.

⚠️ **对比:**
- The room is **clean**.(clean 是形容词:干净的)
- The room is **cleaned** every day.(被动:被打扫)

#### ❌ 错误 3:不及物动词不能被动

不及物动词(没有宾语的动词)**不能用被动语态**!

❌ The accident **was happened** yesterday.
✅ The accident **happened** yesterday.

❌ He **was died** last year.
✅ He **died** last year.

📌 **常见不能被动的动词:** happen, occur, take place, appear, disappear, die, rise, fall, exist, belong, last, remain, seem

## 三、被动语态:特殊用法

### 1️⃣ 双宾语动词的被动

主动:**S + V + IO + DO**(主语 + 谓语 + 间宾 + 直宾)

> Tom **gave me** a book.

可以变两种被动:

> ① **I** was given a book (by Tom).(间宾作主语,常用)
> ② **A book** was given to me (by Tom).(直宾作主语,加 to)

### 2️⃣ 含宾补的被动

主动:**S + V + O + C**(主谓宾补)

> They **made him captain**.(他们选他当队长)
> ↓
> He **was made captain**.(宾补保留)

> She **painted the wall white**.
> ↓
> The wall **was painted white**.

### 3️⃣ 感官 / 使役动词的被动 ⭐

**主动时省略 to,被动时要加回 to!**

| 主动 | 被动 |
|------|------|
| make him **work** | He was made **to work**. |
| see him **leave** | He was seen **to leave**. |
| hear him **sing** | He was heard **to sing**. |
| let him **go** | (let 通常不用被动,用 be allowed to)|

### 4️⃣ 短语动词的被动

短语动词被动时,**整个短语要带过去**(包括介词/副词):

> They **looked after** the baby.
> → The baby **was looked after**.(after 不能丢!)

> They **laughed at** him.
> → He **was laughed at**.

> They **took care of** the dog.
> → The dog **was taken care of**.

### 5️⃣ It is + 过去分词 + that ... 句型 ⭐(雅思写作神器)

```
It is said / believed / reported / known / thought / claimed / assumed + that ...
```

> **It is said that** he is a millionaire.(据说他是百万富翁)
> **It is widely believed that** education changes lives.
> **It is reported that** the storm has caused damage.
> **It is known that** smoking is harmful.

### 6️⃣ 常用被动结构

| 结构 | 例 |
|------|-----|
| **be born**(出生) | I **was born** in 1995. |
| **be made of / from**(由...制成) | This is **made of** wood. |
| **be covered with**(覆盖) | The ground was **covered with** snow. |
| **be filled with**(装满) | The bottle was **filled with** water. |
| **be interested in**(感兴趣) | I am **interested in** music. |
| **be married to**(与...结婚) | She is **married to** Tom. |
| **get + 过去分词**(口语化被动) | He **got hurt**. / She **got married**. |

## 四、虚拟语气:核心概念

### 1️⃣ 什么是虚拟语气?

**英文名:the subjunctive mood。**

**虚拟语气 = 表达非事实、假设、愿望、遗憾、建议或要求,**不是陈述已经确定的事实。

```
事实:I am rich. → 真实陈述
虚拟:If I were rich, I would buy a house. → 假设(我不是富人)
```

### 2️⃣ 虚拟 vs 真实(对比感受)

| 真实 | 虚拟 |
|------|------|
| If it rains, I'll stay home.(可能下雨) | If it rained tomorrow, I would stay home.(觉得不太可能) |
| I am tired. | I wish I were not tired.(我希望我不累,但事实上我累) |
| You should rest.(建议) | I suggest that you rest.(虚拟,后用动词原形) |

### 3️⃣ 别和 would like 混淆 ⭐

`I would like to do something` **通常不算典型"虚拟语气考点"**。它是 `would + 动词` 的**礼貌意愿 / 条件式表达**(polite conditional),用来委婉说"想要":

| 说法 | 语气 |
|------|------|
| I **want** to join. | 直接 |
| I **would like** to join. | 更礼貌、正式 |

真正典型的虚拟条件句是:

> **If** I **had** more time, I **would join**.
> 如果我有更多时间,我就会加入。(暗示:现实中时间不够)

`would like` 的完整用法(结构、与 want 对比、would have liked)见 [[英语助动词与时态系统完全指南#六、情态动词]]。

## 五、虚拟语气:三大条件句 ⭐ 必背!

### 总览表

| 类型 | 时间 | if 从句 | 主句 | 含义 |
|------|------|---------|------|------|
| **真实条件**(0/1类) | 现在/将来(可能) | 一般现在 | will + 原形 | 可能发生 |
| **虚拟现在**(2类) | 现在(假设) | **过去时**(were) | **would + 原形** | 与现在事实相反 |
| **虚拟过去**(3类) | 过去(假设) | **had + 过分** | **would have + 过分** | 与过去事实相反 |
| **虚拟将来** | 将来(假设) | were to / should + 原形 | would + 原形 | 将来不太可能 / 正式预案 |

### 🔑 实用近似规则:"时态退一档"

在 if 引导的**反事实条件句**(counterfactual conditionals)里,可先记:

> **假设的时间点往前退一档。**
> 现在/将来不真实 → 用过去式;过去不真实 → 用过去完成式。

| 你假设的事发生在 | If 从句 | 主句 | 示例 |
|------------------|---------|------|------|
| 现在,但事实相反 | 过去式 / were | would + 原形 | If I **knew**, I **would tell** you. |
| 过去,但事实相反 | had + 过去分词 | would have + 过去分词 | If I **had known**, I **would have told** you. |
| 未来,但认为不太可能 | were to + 原形 / should + 原形 / 有时过去式 | would + 原形 | If it **were to rain**, we **would cancel**. |

⚠️ **适用范围:** 这主要适用于反事实条件句,不是所有"虚拟语气"的万能公式。英语里的 past form 在这里更多是标记**不真实 / 距离感**,未必真的指过去时间。

⚠️ **不要看到 if 就自动退档。** 条件若是现实、开放的,用正常时态:

> If I **have** time tonight, I **will read** the paper.(可行计划)
> If you **heat** ice, it **melts**.(客观规律)
> If the tests **pass**, we **will deploy** tomorrow.(正常项目决策)

### 📌 拆开看结构:had / have 不都是"接原形"

| 结构 | 功能 | 例 |
|------|------|-----|
| have / has + **过去分词** | 现在完成 | I **have studied**. |
| had + **过去分词** | 过去完成 | I **had studied**. |
| would + **动词原形** | 情态后用原形 | I **would study**. |
| would have + **过去分词** | 对过去结果的假设 | I **would have studied**. |

所以 `If I had studied harder, I would have passed.` 拆开是:

| 部分 | 结构 | studied / passed 是什么 |
|------|------|-------------------------|
| If I **had studied** harder | had + 过去分词 | 过去完成式 |
| I **would have passed** | would + have + 过去分词 | 条件完成式 |

这里的 studied / passed **不是"只在虚拟语气里才用的 -ed"**;它们本质是**过去分词**。规则动词的过去式和过去分词碰巧都长成 -ed。不规则动词更清楚:

> If I **had gone** earlier, I **would have caught** the train.
> (go → gone; catch → caught,不是简单加 -ed)

> If she **had seen** the message, she **would have replied**.

📌 **would 后面永远接动词原形:** would do / would cancel;谈过去结果则是 would have done。并非 cancel "例外用原形",而是整条 would 链的规则。

### 1️⃣ 真实条件(可能成真)

```
If + 现在,will + 原形
```

> **If** it **rains**, I **will stay** home.(可能下雨,可能在家)
> **If** you **study** hard, you **will pass**.
> **If** the tests **pass**, we **will deploy** tonight.

⚠️ if 从句里通常**不写 will**:
- ❌ If it **will** rain tomorrow...
- ✅ If it **rains** tomorrow...

### 2️⃣ 虚拟现在(与现在相反)

```
If + 过去时(were!),would / could / might + 原形
```

> **If** I **were** you, I **would** go.(我不是你,这是假设)
> **If** I **had** more time, I **would learn** French.(我没时间)
> **If** he **knew** the truth, he **wouldn't be** so happy.(他不知道)
> **If** I **knew** the answer, I **would tell** you.(现在不知道)
> **If** she **were** here, she **would know** what to do.(她现在不在)
> **If** I **were** you, I **would not deploy** on Friday.

⚠️ **关键:正式/规范英语里,与现在事实相反时常用 were,即使主语是 I / he / she / it!**
- ❌ If I was you, ...
- ✅ If I **were** you, ...(标准虚拟)
- 📌 口语中 was 也可接受,但考试用 were 最稳

### 3️⃣ 虚拟过去(与过去相反)⭐ 雅思高频

```
If + had + 过去分词,would have + 过去分词
```

> **If** I **had studied** harder, I **would have passed**.(我没好好学,我没及格)
> **If** he **had told** me earlier, I **could have helped**.
> **If** they **had not missed** the bus, they **would have arrived** on time.
> **If** we **had tested** the migration, we **would have avoided** the outage.
> **If** I **had known**, I **would have told** you.(日常高频)
> **If** you **had called** me, I **would have helped**.

📌 **关键含义:** 后悔 / 反思 / 假设过去某个改变会带来不同结果。

### 4️⃣ 虚拟将来(低概率 / 正式预案)

```
If + were to + 原形 / should + 原形 / 有时过去式, would + 原形
```

`were to` 不是 we're;写法是 **were to**,约等于"假如 / 万一……发生"。结构是 **were + to + 动词原形**。

> **If** it **were to rain** tomorrow, we **would cancel** the trip.
> **If** the server **were to fail**, we **would switch** to a backup region.
> **If** the company **were to raise** funding, it **would expand** the research team.
> **If** he **were to call** tonight, I **would answer**.
> **If** it **should rain** tomorrow, we **would cancel** the trip.

| 说法 | 语气 |
|------|------|
| If the server **fails**, we **will** switch regions. | 普通、现实的条件句 |
| If the server **were to fail**, we **would** switch... | 更正式,更像 contingency plan |

日常聊天用第一种就够;were to 更像文档、演讲、风险讨论。

### 5️⃣ 混合条件句(关键!)

**条件在过去 + 结果在现在:** 两边按各自指向的时间选形式,不能整句一起"退档"。

```
If + had + 过分,would + 原形(现在)
```

> **If** I **had studied** medicine(过去), I **would be** a doctor now(现在).
> **If** I **had taken** that job, I **would be living** in Beijing now.
> **If** I **had accepted** that job, I **would live** in Singapore now.
> **If** we **had built** observability earlier, we **would be debugging** this much faster now.

### 6️⃣ 倒装(省略 if)⭐ 雅思高分

虚拟条件句中,可以**省略 if,把 were/had/should 提前**:

| 正常 | 倒装 |
|------|------|
| If I **were** you, ... | **Were** I you, ... |
| If I **had known**, ... | **Had** I known, ... |
| If you **should need** help, ... | **Should** you need help, ... |

> **Were** I rich, I would travel the world.
> **Had** I known, I would have come.
> **Should** anything happen, call me.

### 7️⃣ 条件句快速判断

**问自己:这件事有可能发生吗?发生在现在、过去,还是未来?**

| 情境 | 用 |
|------|-----|
| ✅ 可能(真实) | If + 现在,will + 原形 |
| ❌ 现在不可能(假设) | If + 过去(were),would + 原形 |
| ❌ 过去没发生(假设过去) | If + had+过分,would have + 过分 |
| ⚠️ 未来低概率 / 正式预案 | If + were to / should + 原形,would + 原形 |

**口诀:**

```
现在假设:did / were → would do
过去假设:had done → would have done
未来假设:were to do → would do
would 后永远跟原形:would do;过去结果:would have done
```

## 六、虚拟语气:其他高频句型

### 1️⃣ I wish + 虚拟从句 ⭐

**wish 后的从句永远是虚拟!**

| 时间 | 从句结构 | 含义 |
|------|---------|------|
| 现在虚拟 | **过去时**(were) | 希望现在不一样 |
| 过去虚拟 | **had + 过分** | 希望过去不一样(后悔) |
| 将来虚拟 | **would + 原形** | 希望未来发生 |

> I wish I **were** taller.(现在希望)
> I wish I **had studied** harder.(后悔过去)
> I wish I **had studied** computer science earlier.(过去遗憾)
> I wish I **could speak** French.(现在不会)
> I wish you **would stop** complaining.(希望未来)

**简单记:**
- wish + 过去式 → 对现在的非现实愿望
- wish + had + 过去分词 → 对过去的遗憾

📌 **wish vs hope:**
- **hope** + 真实(陈述句)→ I hope you **succeed**.
- **wish** + 虚拟(假设)→ I wish I **were** rich.

### 2️⃣ if only + 虚拟(强调希望)

```
If only + 虚拟从句 = 要是...就好了!
```

> **If only** I **were** ten years younger!
> **If only** I **had known** earlier!
> **If only** he **would call**!

### 3️⃣ as if / as though + 虚拟(好像)

```
as if + 现在虚拟(过去时)= 好像
as if + 过去虚拟(had + 过分)= 好像曾经
```

> He talks **as if** he **knew** everything.(其实他不知道)
> She acted **as if** nothing **had happened**.(其实发生了)

### 4️⃣ It is time + 虚拟(该...的时候了)

```
It is (high / about) time + 主语 + 过去时
```

> **It's time** you **went** to bed.(是时候你睡觉了——你还没睡)
> **It's high time** we **left**.(我们早该走了)

### 5️⃣ would rather + 虚拟(宁愿)

```
would rather + 主语 + 过去时(现在 / 将来不希望)
would rather + 主语 + had+过分(过去后悔)
```

> I **would rather** you **didn't smoke** here.(我希望你别在这抽)
> I **would rather** you **hadn't told** her.(我希望你当初没告诉她)

### 6️⃣ 建议 / 命令动词后的虚拟 ⭐

**结构:** 这些动词后面的 that 从句,用 **(should) + 原形**!

美式英语尤其常见直接用原形;英式英语也可加 should。

#### 建议 / 命令 / 要求类动词:

```
suggest, recommend, propose, advise(建议)
demand, require, request, ask, insist(要求)
order, command(命令)
desire(渴望)
```

> I **suggest** that he **(should) take** a break.(原形,不是 takes!)
> The manager **demanded** that the report **(should) be finished** today.(被动:be finished,不是 is finished)
> The doctor **insisted** that she **(should) stay** in bed.
> They **demanded** that he **(should) leave** immediately.

📌 **It is + 形容词 + that 从句 也用虚拟:**

```
It is essential / important / necessary / vital / urgent + that + 主语 + (should) + 原形
```

> **It is essential** that everyone **(should) be** on time.
> **It is essential** that every user **(should) have** access to the documentation.
> **It is important** that he **(should) understand** the rules.

### 7️⃣ but for / without + 虚拟(要不是)

```
but for / without + 名词 = if it weren't / hadn't been for
```

> **But for** your help, I **would have failed**.(要不是你帮忙)
> **Without** water, plants **would die**.(没水的话)

### 8️⃣ 固定表达(虚拟痕迹) 

有些表达仍保留虚拟语气的痕迹:

| 表达 | 含义 |
|------|------|
| **God bless** you. | 愿上帝保佑你。 |
| **Long live** open source! | 开源万岁! |
| **So be it.** | 就这样吧。 |
| **May** the Force **be** with you. | 愿原力与你同在。 |
| If **need be**, we can postpone the release. | 如有必要,我们可以推迟发布。 |

## 七、雅思高分应用

### 🎯 被动语态在雅思写作中的应用

#### 1. 客观陈述事实(Task 1 / Task 2 高频)

> The graph **was published** in 2020.
> The data **was collected** through interviews.
> Several measures **should be taken** to address the issue.

#### 2. 引述他人观点(避免主观)

> **It is widely believed that** education plays a vital role in society.
> **It is often argued that** technology has both positive and negative effects.
> **It has been suggested that** governments should invest more in renewable energy.

#### 3. 描述变化 / 趋势(Task 1 图表题)

> A significant rise **was observed** between 2010 and 2020.
> The figure **was nearly tripled** over the decade.

### 🎯 虚拟语气在雅思写作中的应用

#### 1. 表达建议 / 提议(Task 2 高分句型)

> It is **essential that** governments **(should) take** immediate action.
> I would **suggest that** schools **(should) implement** more practical courses.

#### 2. 表达假设(议论文中的反驳 / 论证)

> If governments **had invested** more in education, the literacy rate **would have been** much higher.
> **Were** there no internet, communication **would be** far more difficult.

#### 3. 表达愿望(口语高分)

> I **wish** I **had more time** to travel.
> I **wish** I **could speak** more languages fluently.
> **If only** I **had started** learning earlier!

### 🎯 雅思口语高分句型组合

**Part 2 描述经历(用虚拟语气表后悔/反思):**

> If I **had** known about that opportunity, I **would have applied**. Looking back, I really wish I **had taken** more risks when I was younger.

**Part 3 议论(用被动 + 虚拟):**

> It **is often argued that** technology should **be regulated** more strictly. If stricter rules **were imposed**, many of these issues **would be avoided**.

### 🎯 雅思写作 Task 2 万能"虚拟+被动"模板

```
开头(被动客观):
It is widely believed that [topic] plays a crucial role in modern society.

让步(假设/反方):
While some argue that ..., this view fails to consider that ...

支持论据(虚拟假设):
If [假设条件], we would [结果]. For instance, had we invested more in
[X], we would have witnessed [Y].

建议(虚拟建议):
It is essential that governments (should) take measures to ensure that
[期望状态].

结论(被动总结):
In conclusion, it must be acknowledged that [总结观点].
```
