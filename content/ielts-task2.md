## 一、Task 2 评分与基本认知

### 评分四维度(每项 25%)

| 维度 | 考查 |
|------|------|
| **Task Response (TR)** | 是否回答了题目所有部分 + 立场清晰 + 论据充分 |
| **Coherence & Cohesion (CC)** | 段落分明 + 逻辑清晰 + 连接词得当 |
| **Lexical Resource (LR)** | 词汇广 + 准确 + 同义替换 |
| **Grammatical Range (GR)** | 复杂句 + 时态正确 + 错误少 |

### ⚠️ Task 2 七大铁律

1. **必须不少于 250 字**(目标 270-300 字最稳)
2. **必须有清晰立场**(尤其 Agree/Disagree 题)
3. **必须 5 段**(开头 + 2-3 个主体 + 结尾)
4. **每段必须有 Topic Sentence(段首)**
5. **避免重复用词**(important 不能出现 5 次)
6. **观点必须有论据 + 例子**
7. **不要写跑题**(40% 中国考生因跑题失分)

### 📊 时间分配

| 步骤 | 时间 |
|------|------|
| 审题 + 立场 + 列纲 | 5 min |
| 写作 | 30 min |
| 检查 | 5 min |

## 二、4 大题型识别

### 题型 1:Agree / Disagree(同意 / 不同意)

**典型问法:**
- To what extent do you agree or disagree?
- Do you think this is a positive or negative development?
- Is this a good thing or a bad thing?

### 题型 2:Discuss Both Views(讨论双方观点)

**典型问法:**
- Some people think A. Others think B. Discuss both views and give your own opinion.
- Discuss both these views and give your own opinion.

### 题型 3:Problem & Solution(问题与解决)

**典型问法:**
- What problems does this cause? What solutions can you suggest?
- What are the causes of this? How can it be solved?

### 题型 4:Two-part Question(双部分问题)

**典型问法:**
- Why is this happening? Is it a positive or negative development?
- What are the reasons for this? How can the situation be improved?

### 🎯 审题三步法

```
Step 1:圈出关键词(主题 + 限定语)
Step 2:判断题型(看问句最后)
Step 3:决定立场 + 列出 2-3 个论据
```

```

│   - 表明立场 / 概述结构                   │
│                                           │

│   - 解释 / 论证                           │
│   - 举例                                  │
│                                           │
│ Para 3:Body 2(主体 2)                   │ 90 字
│   - Topic sentence                        │
│   - 解释 / 论证                           │
│   - 举例                                  │
│                                           │
```

## 四、题型 1:Agree / Disagree

### 1️⃣ 立场选择

| 立场 | 写法 | 难度 |
|------|------|------|
| **完全同意** | Body 1 + 2 都支持 | 低,但论据少了容易薄 |
| **完全不同意** | Body 1 + 2 都反对 | 同上 |
| **部分同意** | Body 1 同意 + Body 2 反对 | 中,推荐!⭐ |

📌 **推荐"部分同意"** —— 显示思辨能力 + 论据更容易拉满。

### 2️⃣ Agree/Disagree 模板(部分同意版)⭐

```
Para 1(Introduction):

In recent years, [改写题目背景]. While some argue that [一方观点],
others believe [另一方]. In my view, although [让步同意点],
[主立场 - 整体倾向].

──────────────────────────────────

Para 2(Body 1 - 让步同意一面):

It is undeniable that [对方观点有一定道理]. To begin with,
[原因 1]. For instance, [具体例子]. Moreover, [次要原因]. Therefore,
[小结这一面].

──────────────────────────────────

Para 3(Body 2 - 主立场,加倍论证):

However, I am inclined to believe that [自方观点]. The primary reason
is that [核心论据]. Take [具体例子] as a case in point — [展开说].
Furthermore, [第二论据]. As a result, [小结].

──────────────────────────────────

Para 4(Conclusion):

In conclusion, while [让步重申], the more compelling argument is that
[主立场重申]. Therefore, [建议 / 展望 / 总观点].
```

### 3️⃣ Agree/Disagree 实战例题

**题目:** *Some people believe that university students should pay all of their tuition fees themselves. Others think that the government should fund higher education. To what extent do you agree?*

**审题 + 立场:**
- 题型:Agree/Disagree
- 立场:**部分同意政府应承担(政府担主要 + 学生承担一部分)**
- 论据 1(同意政府担):促进教育公平 / 国家发展需要
- 论据 2(同意学生担一部分):培养责任感 / 减轻财政

**完整范文(310 字):**

```
The funding of higher education has long been a contentious issue.
While some maintain that students should bear the full cost of their
tuition, others contend that governments have a responsibility to
finance university education. In my view, although students should
contribute a portion of the costs, the bulk of the funding should
come from the government.

It is true that requiring students to pay tuition fees has certain
benefits. To begin with, when individuals invest their own money in
education, they tend to take their studies more seriously, leading
to better academic outcomes. Moreover, full state funding can place
an enormous strain on national budgets, particularly in countries
with large student populations. For instance, several European nations
have struggled to balance free higher education with other public
spending priorities.

Nevertheless, I firmly believe that government funding should remain
the primary source of financing for higher education. The most
compelling reason is that university education is a public good
that benefits society as a whole, not just the individual student.
Graduates contribute to economic growth, innovation, and the
development of public services such as healthcare and education itself.
Furthermore, charging full tuition risks excluding talented students
from low-income backgrounds, thereby entrenching social inequality.
Countries like Germany and Norway, which offer free higher education,
demonstrate that this approach is both feasible and socially beneficial.

In conclusion, although there is some merit in students contributing
to their tuition, the government should shoulder the majority of
higher education costs. This ensures both academic accessibility
and long-term national development.
```

## 五、题型 2:Discuss Both Views

### 1️⃣ 核心要求

```
1. 必须讨论两方观点(双方都要写,不能偏废)
2. 必须给出自己看法(在引言或结论)
3. 自己的观点 = 与其中一方一致 / 综合
```

### 2️⃣ Discuss Both 模板 ⭐

```
Para 1(Introduction):

[改写背景]. While some people believe that [观点 A], others argue that
[观点 B]. This essay will examine both perspectives before concluding
that [自方立场].

──────────────────────────────────

Para 2(Body 1 - 观点 A):

On the one hand, those who advocate [观点 A] make a valid case. They
argue that [论据 1]. For example, [具体例子]. Additionally, [论据 2].
This view has merit because [总结这一面].

──────────────────────────────────

Para 3(Body 2 - 观点 B):

On the other hand, supporters of [观点 B] also have compelling
arguments. They claim that [论据 1]. A clear example is [例子]. What's
more, [论据 2]. From this standpoint, [小结].

──────────────────────────────────

Para 4(Conclusion + 自方立场):

In conclusion, while both perspectives have valid points, I am more
inclined to support [自方立场] because [核心理由]. [可选:建议或展望].
```

### 3️⃣ Discuss Both 关键词替换

| 表达 | 替换 |
|------|------|
| Some people think | Some argue / Many believe / Advocates of X claim |
| Others think | Critics suggest / Opponents contend / Sceptics maintain |
| For example | For instance / A case in point is / To illustrate |
| Therefore | Thus / Hence / As a result / Consequently |

## 六、题型 3:Problem & Solution

### 1️⃣ 核心结构

```
Para 1:Introduction
Para 2:Problems / Causes
Para 3:Solutions
Para 4:Conclusion
```

### 2️⃣ Problem & Solution 模板

```
Para 1:

[改写题目背景]. This essay will examine the main [problems / causes]
behind this trend and propose practical [solutions / measures] to
address them.

──────────────────────────────────

Para 2(Problems / Causes):

There are several reasons why [现象] has become a serious issue.
The first and most prominent cause is [原因 1]. As [展开]. In
addition, [原因 2]. For instance, [例子]. These factors combine to
create [描述问题严重性].

──────────────────────────────────

Para 3(Solutions):

Fortunately, there are several measures that can be taken to mitigate
this issue. Firstly, [方案 1] should be implemented. This would [解释
效果]. Secondly, governments and individuals should [方案 2]. For
example, [具体例子]. Together, these steps can substantially reduce
[问题].

──────────────────────────────────

Para 4(Conclusion):

In conclusion, while [问题] poses serious challenges, a combination
of [方案概述] can effectively address it. With [展望] efforts, this
trend can be reversed.
```

### 3️⃣ 提出解决方案的常用主体

| 主体 | 措施动词 |
|------|---------|
| 政府(government / authorities) | implement laws / pass regulations / impose taxes / fund programs |
| 学校(schools / universities) | introduce courses / raise awareness / promote |
| 公司(companies / businesses) | adopt green practices / invest in / encourage |
| 个人(individuals / citizens) | reduce consumption / be more responsible / participate in |
| 媒体(the media) | publicise / educate / raise awareness |

📌 **结构小技巧:** Problem 段说 1-2 个主因,Solution 段对应给出 1-2 个方案 → **形成对应**!

## 七、题型 4:Two-part Question

### 1️⃣ 核心要求

题目通常是**两个独立问题**,每个问题用一段回答。

### 2️⃣ Two-part Question 模板

```
Para 1:Introduction
   - 改写背景
   - 简述两个问题
   - 预告结构

Para 2:回答问题 1
   - Topic sentence(直接答)
   - 解释
   - 举例

Para 3:回答问题 2
   - Topic sentence(直接答)
   - 解释
   - 举例

Para 4:Conclusion
   - 综合两问的简短结论
```

### 3️⃣ 实战例题

**题目:** *Many people believe that social media has a negative impact on society. Why do they think so? What can be done to address this?*

**结构:**
- Body 1:为什么人们认为有负面影响(原因)
- Body 2:解决方案

## 八、万能模板段落

### 🎯 引言开头句(2-3 选一)

```
1. 背景式开头:
In recent decades, [话题] has become an increasingly prominent issue
in modern society.

2. 对比式开头:
While some people maintain that [A], others firmly believe that [B].

3. 现象式开头:
It is widely acknowledged that [现象]. This has prompted heated debate
over [核心问题].

4. 数据式开头(慎用,数据要真):
According to recent studies, [数据]. This has raised concerns about
[问题].
```

### 🎯 表明立场句

```
1. In my view, ... / From my perspective, ...
2. I am inclined to believe that ...
3. I firmly side with the latter / former view because ...
4. Personally, I would argue that ...
5. While I acknowledge ..., I ultimately believe ...
```

### 🎯 段首 Topic Sentence 模板

```
1. The most compelling argument in favour of ... is that ...
2. One major benefit / drawback of ... lies in ...
3. The primary reason for this trend / phenomenon is that ...
4. Another significant factor is ...
5. It is also worth noting that ...
```

### 🎯 解释句模板

```
1. This is largely due to the fact that ...
2. The underlying reason is that ...
3. What this means in practice is that ...
4. To put it another way, ...
5. In other words, ...
```

### 🎯 举例句模板

```
1. A clear example of this is ...
2. Take [对象] as a case in point.
3. For instance, ...
4. To illustrate this, consider ...
5. Just look at [国家 / 行业]: ...
```

### 🎯 让步段模板

```
1. Admittedly, [对方观点有道理]. Nevertheless, ...
2. While it is true that [让步], it does not necessarily follow that ...
3. Of course, [对方观点], but [反驳].
4. Critics may argue that [反方], but [回击].
5. Although [反方观点], the benefits outweigh the drawbacks.
```

### 🎯 结论模板

```
1. In conclusion, [立场] because [核心原因 1] and [核心原因 2].
   Therefore, [建议 / 展望].

2. To sum up, while [让步], the case for [立场] is more convincing.
   [展望未来 / 行动呼吁].

3. All things considered, I am of the firm belief that [立场].
   [简短补充想法].
```

### 🎯 论据通用模板(任何话题套用)

#### 经济角度
> ... brings significant economic benefits, including [job creation /
> increased GDP / boosting innovation].

#### 社会角度
> ... promotes social cohesion / reduces inequality / improves quality
> of life for ...

#### 教育角度
> ... fosters critical thinking and broadens horizons, equipping
> individuals with skills for the modern world.

#### 健康角度
> ... contributes to physical and mental well-being, particularly in
> [reducing stress / preventing chronic illnesses].

#### 环境角度
> ... helps mitigate climate change by [reducing emissions / preserving
> biodiversity / promoting sustainability].

#### 个人角度
> ... allows individuals to develop personally, gain confidence, and
> achieve a greater sense of fulfilment.

## 九、Task 2 高分技巧与禁忌

### ⭐ 提分技巧

#### 1. 让步段(Concession)= 7 分必备

**结构:** Although [反方有道理], [自方更有道理].

> Although [opponents argue that university education is not necessary
> for everyone], it remains the most reliable path to upward social
> mobility for the majority.

#### 2. 复杂句穿插

**至少要有:**
- 1 个 although / while 让步从句
- 1 个 if 条件句(虚拟更好)
- 1 个定语从句(which / who / that)
- 1 个 not only ... but also 倒装

#### 3. 同义替换 ⭐

| 高频词 | 替换库 |
|--------|--------|
| good | beneficial / advantageous / favourable / positive |
| bad | detrimental / harmful / adverse / negative |
| big | substantial / considerable / significant / massive |
| small | modest / minimal / negligible / minor |
| important | crucial / vital / essential / pivotal |
| people | individuals / citizens / the public / society |
| think | believe / argue / contend / maintain / hold the view that |
| because | due to / owing to / on account of / as a result of |
| so | therefore / hence / thus / consequently |
| but | however / nevertheless / nonetheless / yet |

#### 4. 高分句型组合

**强调句:**
> **It is** education **that** lays the foundation for economic prosperity.

**倒装句:**
> **Not only** does technology improve efficiency, **but** it **also**
> creates new job opportunities.

**虚拟语气:**
> **If** governments **invested** more in renewable energy, we **would
> witness** a significant reduction in pollution.

**形式主语:**
> **It is essential that** policymakers **(should) take** immediate action.

**让步:**
> **Despite** the obvious benefits, the implementation poses challenges.

### ❌ 高频丢分点

#### 跑题(最致命!)

**审题不准确:**
- 题目问 "to what extent" → 你必须给出"完全 / 部分 / 不"
- 题目限定 "young people" → 你写所有人 → 跑题
- 题目问 "在 X 国" → 你举其他国家例子 → 半跑题

#### 立场不清

❌ "Both sides have good points." (然后没下文 → 失立场分)
✅ "While both have merit, I am more inclined to support X."

#### 论据空洞

❌ "Education is important. Without it, people will fail."
(空洞,无解释,无例子)

✅ "Education forms the cornerstone of economic mobility, providing
individuals with the skills needed to access higher-paying jobs.
For instance, OECD data consistently shows that university
graduates earn significantly more over their lifetimes."

#### 中式英语

| ❌ Chinglish | ✅ Native |
|------------|----------|
| As we all know | It is widely acknowledged |
| With the development of society | In recent decades / In modern society |
| Every coin has two sides | This issue has both positive and negative aspects |
| Last but not least | Finally / Lastly |
| All in all | In conclusion / Overall |
| It is well known that | It is generally accepted that |

⚠️ "As we all know" 在雅思被视为陈词滥调,**直接降分**!

#### 重复用词

❌ Education is important. Education helps people. Education changes lives.

✅ Education is **a fundamental driver** of social progress. **It**
empowers individuals, while **higher learning** in particular opens
doors to opportunities.
